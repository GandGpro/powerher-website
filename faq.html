The event

What is Motherkind PowerHER Fit Fest?

Motherkind PowerHER Fit Fest, in partnership with Investec and Dis-Chem, is a full day of movement, connection and joy, with global fitness icon Kayla Itsines in South Africa for the very first time. There’s a live bootcamp with Kayla, workouts with SA’s top trainers, the Pulse Talks, the Food and Brand Village and the PowerHER After Party.

See the PowerDAY

When and where is it?
  • Johannesburg: Saturday, 7 November 2026 at The Chosen Convention & Expo Centre, 23 Summit Dr, Rispark, Johannesburg South
  • Cape Town: Saturday, 14 November 2026 at Allée Bleue Wine Estate, Cnr R45 & R310, Groot Drakenstein, Franschhoek
What time does it start and end?

The day runs 11:00 – 21:00 in both cities.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. Everyone attending must be 18 or older.

Who’s speaking and performing?

Our PowerVOICES (Pulse Talk speakers) and all-female PowerPERFORMERS will be announced soon. Follow @powerherfitfest to hear first.

Tickets

How do I buy a ticket?

All PowerTICKETS are sold on Howler. Head to our PowerTICKETS page, choose your city and you’ll go straight to the right Howler page.

What’s the difference between General and VVIP?

General gives you the full day: the live bootcamp with Kayla, workouts, talks and sessions all day, your PowerKIT (squat band & TheraBand), the Food and Brand Village, the PowerHER After Party and a 3-month Sweat app subscription.

VVIP adds a meet and photo moment with Kayla, the private VVIP Lounge with a dedicated bar, private bathrooms, a luxury goodie bag from Motherkind and partners, a PowerKIT upgrade (Motherkind yoga mat, PowerHER cap, squat band & TheraBand) and complimentary massages.

    Can I get a refund?

    When you buy, you can add Refund Protect at checkout. It gives you a 100% refund if you can’t attend for one of the many covered reasons. To claim, use the link in your booking confirmation email within 60 days of booking. If you don’t want it, simply untick the box at checkout and you won’t be charged.

    On the day

    Is there parking?

    Yes, parking is available on site at both venues.

    What can’t I bring?

    Please leave these at home: firearms and weapons, umbrellas, camping chairs, cooler boxes, illegal substances and your own alcohol. Right of admission is reserved.

    Can I bring water?

    Yes, you’re welcome to bring one bottle of water.

    Will there be food and drinks?

    Yes. The Food and Brand Village is the place to refuel between sessions. VVIP guests also have a dedicated bar in the private VVIP Lounge.

    What do I get on the day?

    Every PowerTICKET includes a PowerKIT with a squat band and TheraBand. VVIP PowerKITs add a Motherkind yoga mat and a PowerHER cap, plus a luxury goodie bag from Motherkind and partners.
