Motherkind PowerHER is that feeling you get when you’re around women who make you feel good about being exactly who you are.

Motherkind PowerHER Fit Fest, in partnership with Investec and Dis-Chem, is where we move, sweat, learn and dance. But mostly, we make room for ourselves, laugh with our girls and meet women we might never have crossed paths with.
