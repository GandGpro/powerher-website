The PowerHER day

Motherkind PowerHER Fit Fest, in partnership with Investec and Dis-Chem

Motherkind is proud to present PowerHER Fit Fest: a full day of movement, connection and joy, from your first squat with Kayla Itsines to your last dance with your girls.

Get PowerTICKETS

11:00 – 21:00 • Johannesburg & Cape Town

Choose you, because you want to

PowerHER Fit Fest, in partnership with Investec and Dis-Chem, isn’t here to tell you to work harder, become better or prove how strong you are. You do enough of that already.

This is a day to take up space. To try something new. To laugh with your girls. To meet women you might never have crossed paths with. To feel proud of your body for what it can do, and to celebrate yourself and the women around you.

What’s waiting for you

Your day, moment by moment

01 Kayla
The main event

Live bootcamp with Kayla Itsines

You’ve seen her online. Now experience her live. The global fitness icon and woman behind the Sweat app is in South Africa for the very first time, leading one bootcamp with thousands of PowerLADIES moving together.

It’s the moment you’ll talk about for years, and you get to share it with your girls.

02 Move
All day

Workouts with SA’s top trainers

Move all day with some of South Africa’s best. Try something you’ve never done before, find your new favourite, then go again. Every session is open to you, whatever your pace.
